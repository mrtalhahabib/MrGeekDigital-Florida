The Starting Point

The Challenge

Castle & Key had 12 agents, a decade of local goodwill — and a website Google ignored. Their IDX platform auto-generated 40,000+ thin listing pages, Core Web Vitals were deep in the red on mobile, and search traffic had flatlined at ~1,800 visits a month. Every agent also ran their own unclaimed, half-empty Google Business Profile.

The brokerage needed two things at once: a technical rebuild that made the IDX an asset instead of a crawl-budget bonfire, and a local foundation across all 12 agents — Winter Park to Lake Nona — so the brand showed up in map-pack searches at the neighborhood level.
