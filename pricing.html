Pricing Questions

Pricing FAQ

Are there long-term contracts?

No. Every plan is month-to-month. We keep clients with results, not paperwork — and the 90-day ranking guarantee applies to all three plans.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fee on Local Starter or Market Leader. If your site needs a heavy technical foundation sprint (rare, but real), we scope it transparently in your audit before you commit to anything.

When will I see results?

First movement typically inside 60–90 days; meaningful lead growth in months 3–6; compounding after that. Competitive metros (Brickell, downtown Tampa) run longer than secondary markets. Your audit includes an honest timeline for your specific ZIPs.

I’m in a small Florida city — is this overkill?

Smaller markets usually mean faster wins — Local Starter in a place like Ocala or Panama City can dominate quickly because competitors publish almost nothing. You might be surprised how cheaply you can own a small city.

Do you work with my brokerage’s website?

Yes — kvCORE, Real Geeks, Sierra Interactive, WordPress + IDX Broker and Placester, plus most brokerage template sites. Some platforms lock more doors than others; the audit tells you exactly what’s possible on yours.

What does reporting look like?

Rankings and leads reported monthly on Starter, bi-weekly on Market Leader, weekly with a live dashboard on Luxury & Multi-City. Always in plain English — calls, leads and positions you can verify, not impression charts.
