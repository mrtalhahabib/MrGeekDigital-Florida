FAQ

Questions Agents Ask Us

Are these links safe?

100% white-hat: news features, chambers, legitimate directories, sponsorships and expert quotes. Nothing rented, nothing from link networks, nothing that puts your domain at risk. We’ve never had a client penalized — and we intend to keep it that way.

How many links will I get per month?

Typically 4–10 quality placements. One feature in a Florida business journal outweighs fifty junk directory links — we optimize for impact, not invoice padding.

Can I approve every placement?

Yes. You’ll see every URL, every anchor text and every source in your monthly report — and you can veto anything before it goes live.

Do sponsorship costs cost extra?

Sponsorship budgets themselves (youth leagues, charity events) are separate from service fees. We source and negotiate the opportunities; you approve the budget.

Will links alone get me to #1?

Links are fuel, not the engine. They multiply everything else — which is why most clients run this alongside Local SEO or Neighborhood Content for the compounding effect.
