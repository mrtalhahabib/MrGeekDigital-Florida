FAQ

Questions Agents Ask Us

Do you work with my platform?

kvCORE, Real Geeks, Sierra Interactive, WordPress + IDX Broker and Placester — yes. Every platform gives us different room to work; some are more locked down than others. The free audit tells you exactly what’s possible on yours.

Will SEO changes break my IDX search?

No. We work within your platform’s structure. Speed and indexation improvements make the home search faster and more visible — never broken.

Should I just rebuild my website instead?

Usually no. Most agent sites need surgery, not a funeral. If a rebuild genuinely is the smarter path — some platforms simply can’t be saved — we’ll tell you straight, before you spend another dollar.

How many of my listing pages can actually rank?

We don’t chase every auto-generated listing page. We index the ones with real search demand — buildings, communities, property types — and shield the rest from diluting your site. The audit maps exactly where that line sits in your market.

My site is brand new. Too early for SEO?

Perfect timing. Foundations are far cheaper to lay now than to retrofit after two years of bad structure.
