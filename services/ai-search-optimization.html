FAQ

Questions Agents Ask Us

What exactly are AEO and GEO?

Answer Engine Optimization and Generative Engine Optimization: the discipline of getting AI tools — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — to recommend and cite you. Think of it as SEO for machines that answer instead of list.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT recommends me?

No — and nobody honestly can. What we do is maximize every signal AI models use to choose names (entity strength, reviews, structured content, citations) and then measure the results weekly. You’ll see your share of answers climb.

Does this replace regular SEO?

No — it’s built on it. AI recommendations lean on the same fundamentals: strong entity, great reviews, structured local content. Most clients run AI Search alongside Local SEO or Neighborhood Content.

How fast do results show?

First movement in AI answers typically appears within 60–90 days of entity unification and content publishing. AI share compounds from there.

Which AI tools do you track?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews — checked weekly against your actual buyer queries in your actual cities.
