The Problem

Where Florida Profiles Leak Leads

Most agents have a half-filled profile and wonder why the phone is quiet.

01

Wrong or missing categories.

‘Real estate agent’ vs. ‘Real estate agency’ vs. ‘Real estate consultant’ — Google reads these very differently. An Orlando agent selling Lake Nona new construction needs a different category stack than a Winter Park luxury specialist. Most are set wrong on day one.

02

Stale photos and dead posts.

Buyers judge your profile in three seconds. If your last photo is from 2023 and your last post is a canned holiday graphic, they tap the next agent — usually your competitor.

03

Reviews trickle in — unanswered.

Review count and velocity are ranking factors, and unanswered reviews (even the 5-star ones) waste trust. Meanwhile the agent across town asks at every closing table.

FAQ

Questions Agents Ask Us

Isn’t my Google profile just… there?

An unclaimed or half-filled profile quietly leaks calls to whoever optimized theirs. Your GBP is a ranking factor in the map pack and often the first — and only — thing a buyer sees before calling. It deserves strategy, not autopilot.

I’m on a team. Should my profile rank or my broker’s?

Both — with different jobs. Your profile builds your book of business; your brokerage’s profile supports it. We structure both so they help, not compete.

Can you get me more reviews without breaking Google’s rules?

Yes. Timing (the closing table), scripts, QR codes and follow-up sequences grow reviews honestly. We never incentivize or gate reviews — Google penalizes that, and we won’t risk your profile.

How often will you post to my profile?

Weekly — market updates, new listings, sold stories and neighborhood spotlights. Local Signals posts are a direct ranking input and keep your profile alive for buyers.

My listings change constantly. How do photos keep up?

We give you (or your TC) a 10-minute weekly photo routine. We handle optimization, geo-context and posting. Fresh, real, local — never stock.
