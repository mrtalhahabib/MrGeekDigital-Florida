Our Story

We Started Generic. We Chose This.

MrGeek began as a full-service digital agency — websites, ads, social, SEO, everything for everyone. We were competent at all of it and remarkable at none of it. And we kept meeting the same client: a Florida real estate agent spending $2,000–$4,000 a month renting leads from portals, working evenings to convert shared Zillow inquiries, and getting quoted six-month contracts by agencies who couldn’t name three neighborhoods in the agent’s own farm area.

So we made a decision most agencies say they make and almost none keep: we fired every other industry. No more spreading focus across plumbers and law firms. Real estate agents became the only client we serve, and Florida — with its snowbird calendars, hurricane-shifted search behavior, condo towers, waterfront rules and 55+ communities — became the only market we study.

That was 120+ agents ago. Today we track 67 counties weekly, rank agents in every major metro from Pensacola to Key West, and still turn down non-real-estate work every month. It’s not a niche marketing line. It’s the whole operating model.
